Lead story · The Hague · Justice

World Court: Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya

The International Court of Justice ruled on Thursday that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in 2017 — the first time in its 80-year history the World Court has held a state responsible for genocide.

By nine votes to six, the judges found the military’s “clearance operations” in northern Rakhine State killed about 10,000 Rohingya. The court ordered Myanmar to pay reparations and allow refugees to return home.

China · Diplomacy

Trump, Xi and Putin hold first three-way summit

The three leaders met in Shenzhen on Thursday for about 80 minutes and issued a four-point “Declaration toward Peace”: a nuclear war “cannot be won and must never be fought”. It said nothing about Ukraine or the war with Iran.

Middle East · Energy

Tankers hit again in the Strait of Hormuz

Iran said on Thursday that tankers struck naval mines in the strait, and a fresh projectile strike on a tanker was reported on Wednesday evening. Two seafarers are missing this week after attacks on merchant shipping, including 11 injured aboard an ADNOC-linked tanker.

United States · Politics

Senate rejects bid to curb Trump’s Iran war powers

The US Senate voted 51–48 on Thursday to reject a war powers resolution that would have directed President Trump to end the war in Iran or seek Congress’s approval. Three Republicans backed it; one Democrat opposed it.

Asia · Bangladesh

Violence spreads as Bangladesh locks down

Supporters of the banned Awami League staged a nationwide lockdown on Thursday. The ancestral home of former leader Muhammad Yunus was set on fire, two ruling-party members were killed, and unrest hit 28 districts.
